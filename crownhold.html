Controls

Overview (commander)

  • WASD / arrows pan, QE rotate, wheel zoom, right-drag orbit, middle-drag pan
  • Left click / drag: select your units. Shift adds to the selection
  • Right click: move & attack there, attack an enemy, or follow a friendly unit
  • C or Tab: take control of the selected unit (any unit, even the King)
  • H hold position, F follow the nearest hero (or the hovered unit)
  • Ctrl+1-9 save a control group, 1-9 recall it. Ctrl+A all soldiers, K King, J heroes
  • B build panel. Pick a building, R rotates, click places. Drag to lay walls. Right click / Esc cancels
  • Click a building to repair, upgrade or sell it (Del). R repairs everything, or tick auto-repair. Engineers repair for free
  • N / Space next wave, P pause, = fast forward, M mute
  • Shift+click a recruit buys five. Select a Barracks or Tavern and press "Muster recruits here" to choose where they appear

Possessed unit (first person)

  • Mouse look (click the game once to capture the mouse), WASD move, arrow keys turn, Shift sprint, O opens settings (mouse sensitivity, raw input, invert, volume, shadows)
  • LMB attack (hold to keep swinging / shooting)
  • Q E hero abilities, aimed where you look
  • RMB tap or G: send your soldiers to attack where you aim. Without mouse capture, holding RMB and dragging looks around without limits
  • F soldiers follow you, H hold, V defend the King
  • T toggle third person, Tab / Esc back to the overview
  • Soldiers selected before taking control become your squad. Otherwise you command all of them

Tips

  • Between waves you build and repair instantly. During a wave a new building is only a construction site until an Engineer finishes it (or the wave ends), and only Engineers can repair
  • Enemies arrive only along the map's lanes (ringed on the minimap), wind through two belts of terrain, then cross open ground to the castle. They walk a long way round walls before chewing through, so walls steer them into your towers
  • High-ground summits beside the lanes take a tower each: +30% range, +15% damage, and melee cannot reach them (artillery, archers and harpies can)
  • Wonders at the bottom of the Build panel are ruinously expensive and absurdly strong, from a treasury that pays interest up to the Throne of Ages
  • The wave preview names the wave's modifier (night, fog, frenzy, swarm, siege...) and its contract, a goal paid to every defender. Elites wear a coloured ring and pay double. Heroes level up from their kills
  • King's errands: a beam of light marks something far away that only the King can fetch, reach or work on. Optional, gone when the wave ends, and richly rewarded
  • The Keep shelters units inside from ranged attacks, and units inside cannot shoot out either. The King fights back if enemies get in
  • Catapults out-range your towers: send a hero or cavalry of swordsmen to kill them
  • A fallen hero stays dead until you buy them back; there is only ever one of each hero. A fallen King ends the game
