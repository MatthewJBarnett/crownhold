Controls

Overview (commander)

  • WASD / arrows pan, QE rotate, wheel zoom, right-drag orbit, middle-drag pan
  • Left click / drag: select your units. Shift adds to the selection
  • Right click: move & attack there, attack an enemy, or follow a friendly unit
  • C or Tab: take control of the selected unit (any unit, even the King)
  • H hold position, F follow the nearest hero (or the hovered unit)
  • Ctrl+1-9 save a control group, 1-9 recall it. Ctrl+A all soldiers, K King, J heroes
  • B build panel. Pick a building, R rotates, click places. Drag to lay walls. Right click / Esc cancels
  • Click a building to repair, upgrade or sell it (Del). R repairs everything, or tick auto-repair. Engineers repair for free
  • N / Space next wave, P pause, = fast forward, M mute

Possessed unit (first person)

  • Mouse look (click the game once to capture the mouse), WASD move, arrow keys turn, Shift sprint, [ ] mouse sensitivity (or the slider below)
  • LMB attack (hold to keep swinging / shooting)
  • Q E hero abilities, aimed where you look
  • RMB tap or G: send your soldiers to attack where you aim. Without mouse capture, holding RMB and dragging looks around without limits
  • F soldiers follow you, H hold, V defend the King
  • T toggle third person, Tab / Esc back to the overview
  • Soldiers selected before taking control become your squad. Otherwise you command all of them

Tips

  • Between waves you build and repair instantly. During a wave a new building is only a construction site until an Engineer finishes it (or the wave ends), and only Engineers can repair
  • Enemies path to the King and will break through the cheapest walls in their way. Funnel them into your gate and tower fire
  • The Keep shelters units inside from ranged attacks. The King fights back if enemies get in
  • Catapults out-range your towers: send a hero or cavalry of swordsmen to kill them
  • Fallen heroes return after the wave. A fallen King ends the game
100% Raw input ignores the system pointer speed and acceleration, like Minecraft. Turn it off if the view feels far faster or slower than your cursor.
